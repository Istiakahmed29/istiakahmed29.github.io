About

A hospitality mind with an operator's discipline

I grew up in Jessore, Bangladesh, and studied Tourism & Hospitality Management at Gopalganj Science and Technology University — not because it sounded glamorous, but because I've always been curious about how experiences are built: the flight, the hotel, the itinerary, the small details guests never notice but always feel.

During my degree I worked. I managed social media for a company, ran digital marketing inside a travel agency, and supported operations at a youth-led non-profit. Along the way I picked up practical tools — Sabre GDS ticketing, Excel and VBA, data analysis, content design — the kind that let me contribute from day one instead of needing months of ramp-up.

What drives me is the intersection: tourism knowledge, business thinking, and digital craft. I like work where analysis meets people — planning a destination, running a campaign, keeping an operation moving.
