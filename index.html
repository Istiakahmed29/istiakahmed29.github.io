My story

I'm Istiak Ahmed Badhon, a BBA graduate in Tourism & Hospitality Management from Gopalganj Science and Technology University, based in Jessore, Bangladesh.

My path so far has moved between three worlds: marketing travel experiences at Make My Holidays and Accesshive, keeping operations running at YSSE, and making sense of data with Excel and VBA. What ties them together is a simple belief — that good tourism is built on understanding people, and good business is built on understanding numbers.

Next, I want to grow into operations and strategic planning in tourism and hospitality — and I'm open to roles where marketing, data and guest experience meet.
